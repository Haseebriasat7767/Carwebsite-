Services we are known for

Six of the jobs that fill our diary every week. Tap a category to see what the work actually involves.

Diagnostics

Computer Scanning & Diagnostic Testing

A fault code tells you which circuit complained, not what went wrong. The same code can be caused by a failed sensor, damaged wiring, a weak power supply, a software issue or a mechanical problem elsewhere. Reading codes is the starting point; interpreting them is the skill.

  • Full-system scanning: engine, gearbox, ABS, airbag, climate, body modules
  • Live data logging and freeze-frame analysis
  • Oscilloscope testing of sensors, injectors and ignition signals
  • Voltage drop, resistance and continuity checks on wiring
  • Actuator and component activation tests
  • Coding, adaptation and calibration procedures

Mechanical

Engine Repair, Overhaul and Rebuilds

Engines fail for reasons, not by accident. Overdue oil changes, a cooling system that has quietly lost efficiency in Gulf heat, a stretched timing chain, or a small leak that turned into a big one. Our job is to establish the actual cause before recommending anything, because rebuilding an engine that only needed a coil pack wastes your money and our reputation.

  • Misfire diagnosis: coils, injectors, plugs, compression, valve timing
  • Timing chain and belt replacement with tensioner and guide sets
  • Cylinder head gasket replacement, head skimming and pressure testing
  • Overheating investigation: thermostat, water pump, radiator, fans
  • Oil consumption and PCV system repairs, seal and gasket renewal
  • Turbocharger diagnosis, replacement and oil feed line work

Maintenance

Oil Change, Filters & Fluid Service

An oil change looks like the simplest job in the workshop, but the specification matters more than most people realise. Viscosity, additive package and approval standard all differ between engines, and the wrong oil can affect variable valve timing, turbo bearing life and emissions systems.

  • Engine oil to manufacturer specification and correct capacity
  • Oil filter, sump plug washer and drain procedure
  • Air filter and cabin filter inspection or replacement
  • Top-up of coolant, brake fluid, washer fluid and power steering
  • Tyre pressures, tread depth and visual condition check
  • Brake pad and disc thickness measurement

Safety

Tyres, Wheel Balancing & Alignment

Tyres are the only contact your car has with the road, and Gulf conditions are hard on them. Extreme surface temperatures, sun exposure and abrasive dust age rubber faster than mileage alone would suggest, which is why a tyre with legal tread can still be unsafe at five years old.

  • Tyre supply and fitting for cars, SUVs and light commercial vehicles
  • Computerised wheel balancing and road-force correction
  • Four-wheel alignment with printed before-and-after report
  • Puncture repair to recognised standards where safe
  • Tyre rotation and pressure checks
  • TPMS sensor replacement, programming and reset

Mechanical

Gearbox & Transmission Repair

Transmission faults are among the most misunderstood problems we see. A car that 'jerks at low speed' might have degraded fluid, a failing solenoid, a worn torque converter, a software adaptation issue or an engine misfire being mistaken for a gearbox fault. Correct diagnosis matters because the repair options vary enormously in cost.

  • ATF drain-and-fill and full flush with correct fluid specification
  • Filter, pan gasket and adapter seal replacement
  • Valve body, solenoid pack and mechatronic unit repair
  • Torque converter diagnosis, replacement and lock-up clutch repair
  • CVT belt and pulley inspection, fluid service and repairs
  • Dual-clutch gearbox service, clutch pack and actuator work
Full service details Book on WhatsApp
Electrical

Battery Testing, Replacement & Charging System Repair

Heat is the main killer of car batteries. Under-bonnet temperatures in a Dubai summer accelerate plate corrosion and fluid loss, and a battery that tests fine in spring can fail without warning in July. Most batteries here last two to three years rather than the five claimed on the box.

  • Conductance and load testing with printed results
  • Replacement with the correct specification and capacity
  • AGM and EFB batteries for start-stop vehicles
  • Battery registration and coding where required
  • Alternator output, diode ripple and belt condition checks
  • Starter motor current draw testing and replacement

What separates a good independent workshop

When something goes wrong with your car, the choice is usually between a franchised dealership and an independent workshop. For anything outside warranty, the independent route is normally faster and considerably cheaper — provided the workshop has the right equipment, the right technical information and technicians who work on your type of car regularly. That last point is where most cheap garages fall down.

We match the technician to the vehicle rather than treating every car as interchangeable. Oil services, brake work, engine diagnostics, gearbox repairs, air conditioning faults and collision bodywork are all handled here, with pricing agreed in writing before a single bolt is undone.

Maintenance built for Gulf conditions

Heat, dust and stop-start traffic age components faster than the service intervals written for temperate climates. Coolant degrades, rubber perishes, batteries lose capacity and air conditioning systems work far harder than their designers expected. We specify fluids and parts for the conditions your car actually operates in, and we adjust intervals accordingly.

Luxury, performance and electrified vehicles

European luxury cars, performance models, hybrids and electric vehicles all need manufacturer-level diagnostic access, correct torque data and, in the case of high-voltage systems, safe isolation procedures. We invest in that tooling and training rather than sending these cars away.

Air conditioning, brakes and electrics: the three most requested jobs

Refrigerant leaks, compressors and condensers; pads, discs, fluid and ABS faults; batteries, alternators, wiring and modules. These are the systems the climate punishes hardest, so we stock the common parts and keep the equipment calibrated to find faults quickly instead of guessing.

Frequently asked questions

The questions we get asked most often, answered plainly.

Which services does the workshop provide?

Mechanical repair and servicing, engine and gearbox work, computer diagnostics, air conditioning, brakes, suspension and steering, batteries and auto electrics, tyres, body repair and painting, pre-purchase inspections, plus 24/7 roadside assistance and recovery across Dubai.

How do I book an appointment?

Message us on WhatsApp, call the workshop number, or fill in the estimate form on any page and we will confirm a slot. Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours, although booked cars are always seen first.

Is roadside assistance really available around the clock?

Yes. Recovery crews operate 24 hours a day, seven days a week, covering towing, jump-starts, flat tyres, fuel delivery, lockouts, winch recovery and accident recovery anywhere in Dubai.

Do you work on luxury, hybrid and electric cars?

We do. Our technicians are trained on brand-specific diagnostic systems and high-voltage safety procedures, so European luxury cars, hybrids and EVs are handled by people who work on them regularly.

Can a mechanic come to my home or office?

Yes — our mobile mechanic service covers many diagnostics, batteries, brakes, filters, tyres and minor repairs on site. Anything needing a lift or specialist equipment is collected and returned by recovery.

Will using an independent workshop affect my warranty?

In most cases, no. Routine maintenance carried out to manufacturer specification with OEM-grade parts and documented properly does not by itself invalidate a new-car warranty in the UAE. We keep detailed service records for every visit.

How long does a typical job take?

Servicing, brakes, batteries and tyres are usually same-day. Air conditioning repairs, gearbox work and diagnostics that depend on parts can take one to three days. We give you a realistic timeframe when you approve the quote.

Which areas of Dubai do you cover?

Our workshop is in Al Quoz with easy access from Sheikh Zayed Road, and our mobile crews cover the whole emirate — Marina, JBR, Downtown, Business Bay, JVC, Motor City, Palm Jumeirah, Bluewaters, Nad Al Sheba, Mirdif, Deira and beyond.
