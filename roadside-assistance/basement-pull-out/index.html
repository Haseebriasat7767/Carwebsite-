Basement Pull-Out

What we do and how we do it

Dubai's towers and malls put thousands of cars in underground parking with height limits of two metres or less, tight ramps and pillars that prevent a recovery truck from getting anywhere near. A car that will not start down there needs specialist extraction.

We use low-profile units, winch systems, skates and protective equipment to roll or pull the vehicle out without damaging the car, the ramp, the columns or the building's flooring. Building management usually need to be informed, and we handle that coordination.

Basement recovery includes

  • Extraction from car parks with low ceiling clearance
  • Winch and skate systems for non-rolling vehicles
  • Steering and braking control during extraction
  • Protection of floors, ramps, pillars and the vehicle
  • Coordination with building security and management
  • Battery jump-start attempts before extraction
  • On-site diagnosis where space allows
  • Transfer to flatbed once clear of the structure

Typical basement recovery calls

  • Car will not start after days parked underground
  • Battery flat with no access for jump leads
  • Vehicle stuck in gear or with a failed parking brake
  • Tyre or wheel damage preventing the car rolling
  • Gearbox failure in a tight parking space
  • Long-term parked cars that have seized up

Questions about basement pull-out

Do you need permission from building management?

Usually yes, and we will liaise with security or facilities staff before starting. Having the vehicle registration and the owner's ID ready speeds this up considerably.

Can a car with a seized parking brake be moved?

Yes, using skates or wheel dollies under the locked wheels, with careful controlled pulling. We assess on arrival and choose the safest method.

How long does basement extraction take?

Between 30 minutes and an hour once on site, depending on access, ceiling height, whether the car rolls and how much coordination the building requires.
